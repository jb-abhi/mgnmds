Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

MGN Motor Driving School ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information when you visit our website or contact us about our driving courses.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us or enquire about our services:

  • Contact information: Your name, phone number, and email address, if provided.
  • Enquiry details: The course you are interested in and any information you include in your message.
  • Technical information: Basic information such as browser type, device information, and website usage data, where collected through hosting, security, or analytics services.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through our website enquiry form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and requests.
  • Explain our driving courses, fees, and services.
  • Contact you regarding your enquiry or requested services.
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • Protect our website against spam, misuse, and security threats.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We will not use your contact details for unrelated promotional communications without an appropriate lawful basis or consent, where required.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate our website, securely store enquiry submissions, or maintain website security. These providers may include website hosting and database services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect our legal rights and the safety of others.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information submitted through our website against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

If our website uses third-party infrastructure, such as Cloudflare for hosting or Supabase for database services, information may be processed or stored through those providers, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide requested services, maintain relevant business records, resolve disputes, or meet applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or otherwise dispose of it securely, subject to applicable retention obligations.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use essential technologies required for its operation, security, and performance.

If analytics tools, advertising technologies, or non-essential cookies are introduced, this policy will be updated as appropriate, and any required consent will be obtained.

7. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external services, including WhatsApp, telephone links, or third-party websites.

When you use these services, their own terms and privacy policies may apply. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to us. You may also raise concerns about how your information is used.

We will review and respond to such requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization where required by law. If you believe a child has submitted personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal obligations change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
