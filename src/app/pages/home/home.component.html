About {{ s.name }}

At MGN Motor Driving School, we're here to help you build confidence, develop safe driving habits, and become a responsible driver for life.

Legacy

Every journey has a beginning, and our old driving-school vehicle holds a special place in our story. It serves as a reminder of the journey behind us and the experience gained along the way.

Objective

Located in Pedikulam, Pulimath, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, we focus on preparing learners for everyday driving—from mastering traffic rules to recognising road hazards and responding calmly to unexpected situations.

Why Choose Us

Why learners choose us

At MGN Motor Driving School, we go beyond basic test routes to train you directly on Thiruvananthapuram’s narrow lanes, slopes, and unpredictable traffic. Our patient, step-by-step guidance instills calm instincts and hazard awareness, ensuring you feel completely self-reliant behind the wheel from day one.
